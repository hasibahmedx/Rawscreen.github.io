From rushes to render

Every project runs in this order
Stage 1 / Ingest

Sync and sort

You send footage. We make proxies, sync audio, log selects and agree the brief before the first cut.

Stage 2 / Assembly

Rough cut

The full story in order, with placeholder music and no polish. You review structure, not details.

Stage 3 / Fine cut

Pace and picture lock

Trims, transitions, graphics and cutaways. Two feedback rounds are included, then the picture locks.

Stage 4 / Finish

Grade and mix

Colour, sound and final graphics are applied to the locked picture so nothing shifts afterwards.

Stage 5 / Deliver

Export for every screen

Master file plus platform versions, subtitles and thumbnails, delivered by link.
